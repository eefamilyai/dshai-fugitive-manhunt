Privacy Policy

App: Fugitive (package com.jaryl.fugitive) · Last updated: 25 October 2026

Fugitive is a location-based party game. To play, your device shares its location with the other players in your game. This policy explains exactly what is collected, why, who can see it, and how long it is kept. We do not sell data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking SDKs.

2. When location is collected

Location is only collected while you are in a lobby or game. During a game, Fugitive runs a foreground service with a visible, persistent notification so location sharing continues when your screen is off. When the game ends or you leave the lobby, location collection stops. Fugitive does not request background location permission and never collects location when you are not playing.

3. Practice mode

Practice games against bots run entirely on your device. No data is sent anywhere.

4. Service providers

Online games use Google Firebase (Realtime Database and Anonymous Authentication) to relay game data between players. Data is processed under Google's Firebase privacy terms. Map tiles are downloaded from OpenFreeMap; like any website, the tile server receives your IP address and the map area being viewed, but not your identity or exact position. Optional QR-code scanning uses the Google Play services code scanner, which processes camera frames on-device.

5. Security

All network traffic is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Database security rules ensure only members of a lobby can read it, and each player can only write their own location.

6. Your choices

  • You can deny location permission; you can still use the virtual joystick in practice mode.
  • Leave a lobby at any time to stop sharing and remove your data from it.
  • Clearing the app's data or uninstalling removes your local profile and anonymous ID.
  • To request deletion of any data, email the address below.

7. Children

Fugitive is not directed at children under 13. Players should agree on safe play areas and never play near traffic.
